Using handwritten notes

Turn checked, readable text into a quiz. For handwritten pages, the recommended free option is to copy text with your phone’s scanner, paste it here, and correct any mistakes.

Works best

  • Typed or pasted notes that you have checked for accuracy.
  • At least four different terms, each with a definition on its own line.
  • Clear printed photos with good lighting and no glare.

Not reliable

  • Automatic reading of full handwritten notebook pages.
  • Messy cursive, faint writing, shadows, blur, or tilted photos.
  • Diagrams, tables, and notes without clear term-and-definition pairs.

Recommended: scan, paste, review

  1. Copy the writing. On Samsung / Android, open your photo in Gallery or Google Photos and try Extract text or Lens → Text → Copy. On iPhone, use Live Text in Photos. Availability and accuracy vary.
  2. Paste and correct. Choose Create My Own Quiz, open Paste scanned text or type study notes, and paste. Compare every line with your original photo.
  3. Format your notes. Use at least four distinct terms in the format below. If scanning fails, type the notes or choose Write my own questions.
  4. Create and check your quiz. Tap Create My Game, review the questions and all answer choices, then tap Play My Game.

About the experimental handwriting button: A clean handwritten line worked in testing; a wider handwritten photo failed. For the best chance, try a close, upright photo of a few clearly separated lines on a plain background. The first download is large, and some phones may be slow or unable to run it. It can miss words or invent text. Check every word in the review box before choosing Use these corrected notes.
